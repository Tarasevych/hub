# Tests

## Test 1 - Minimal signed-out shell and account boundary [sanity]
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: signed-out shell rendering, account menu boundary, no blank canvas before auth
Description: A signed-out visitor sees a real shell (not a blank screen) and cannot reach authenticated-only areas.

Steps:
1. Open the app while signed out
2. Confirm the boot shell or signed-out shell content is visible (not a blank page)
3. Confirm identity, mail and admin areas are not reachable without signing in

Expected: The signed-out visitor sees a real, non-blank shell and account-only areas stay out of reach.
